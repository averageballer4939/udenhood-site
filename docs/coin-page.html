Coin pages

A coin and the proof card of the repo it names, read from the chain and from GitHub each time the page opens.

Open Coins and press a coin, or open coin.html?t= followed by its address: the page reads the chain, then checks the repo the coin names.

What the page reads

On the pageRead from
Name, ticker, image, text, linksthe coin's own contract, as its creator wrote them at launch
“Launched by” and “Creator tax”Pons's record of that coin
“On the curve”the coin's curve: ETH raised, of the ETH needed to leave the curve
“Repo”the last line of the coin's text, Repo: github.com/owner/repo
“Market cap” and “Price”worked out from what the coin's curve holds, in ETH
The chart and “Trades”the buys and sells the coin's curve recorded on the chain

The four lights of that repo then run as they do on Check, with one difference: Owner knows the coin.

A coin without the repo line. Any coin launched on Pons has a page here. If its text names no repo the page says “This coin names no GitHub repo, so there is nothing to check.”

Owner, from the coin's side

The lamp is fully on when four things agree:

  • the coin names the repo;
  • the repo's udenhood.json names a wallet;
  • that wallet is the one that launched the coin;
  • the ticker in the file is the coin's ticker, and the coin the file names is this one.

Anyone can launch a coin that names a repo they do not own. The repo's file will not name their wallet, and the lamp stays off: “The coin and the repo do not match.” Every line of the lamp.

The Coins list

Nobody at Udenhood picks the coins. The list is rebuilt in your browser each time you open it:

  1. GitHub is asked

    for public repos that carry the topic udenhood. Forks and archived repos are left out. The 30 most recently updated are read.

  2. Each repo's file is read

    A repo enters only if its udenhood.json names a wallet and a coin.

  3. The coin is read on the chain

    It must be a coin launched on Pons.

ColumnIt shows
Owner“Signed” when the Owner lamp is fully on, “No match” when it is not
Last pushdays since the repo's last push. The lamp follows the Shipping rule
On the curveETH raised, of the ETH needed to leave the curve

Signed coins come first, then the most recent push. Open shows the full card.

A new coin can take a few minutes. GitHub needs time to list a repo under a new topic, and its raw file server can serve an older file for up to five minutes.

The chart and the trades

The chart is drawn in your browser from the trades of the coin's curve. 1m, 5m, 15m and 1h set the length of a candle. Mcap and Price switch between the market cap and the price of one coin, both in ETH.

Under it, “Trades” lists the latest buys and sells, each with its wallet.

A coin nobody traded yet. The chart says “No trade yet. The first buy draws the first candle.”

Buy and sell

The panel trades on the coin's Pons curve, from the wallet you are logged in with. Udenhood takes no fee and holds nothing.

  1. Pick Buy or Sell

    Buy takes an amount in ETH. Sell takes an amount of the coin, or a share of what you hold.

  2. Read the quote

    “You get”, “Minimum, after tolerance”, “Price impact”, “Pons trading fee”, “Creator tax” and “Network fee, estimate” follow what you type. “Price tolerance” sets how far the price may move before the trade is refused.

  3. Press the button

    The trade is replayed against the chain first: “Checking the trade against the chain”. Then your wallet opens. Logged out, the same button opens the log in window.

  4. Follow it

    “Signed in your wallet”, “Sent to Robinhood Chain”, “Confirmed on chain”.

The first sale of a coin asks twice. The coin has to allow its curve to take your coins. That allowance is one confirmation, the sale is a second one. The allowance is given once per coin and has no limit, as on Pons's own site.

No receipt yet is not a failure. “Not confirmed yet. It can still go through: follow the transaction before you try again.”

A coin that raised enough leaves its curve for a pool. The panel then says “This coin left its curve. It trades in a pool now.” and Trade on Pons takes you there.

The buttons

  • Trade on Pons opens the same coin on Pons's site.
  • Copy copies the coin's address.
  • Explorer opens the coin on the chain explorer.

When the page is empty

LineWhy
“This page needs a coin address.”the link has no address after ?t=
“This address is not a coin launched on Pons.”Pons has no record of that address
“The chain node did not answer.”the public node is busy. Press Try again
“The repo this coin names is not public, or does not exist.”the repo was renamed, deleted or made private

What the page does not prove

  • The image and the text are the creator's. The image is served from where the creator put it. Udenhood does not host or review either.
  • Not advice. The chart shows past trades on the curve. It says nothing about what the coin will be worth. A coin can go to zero.
  • Not more than the card. Four lights on say the project exists, runs and ships. What the card does not prove.
