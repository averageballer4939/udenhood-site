Getting started

How Udenhood works.

A coin should come from a repo that signed it. Four lights show if the project behind it exists, runs and ships.

Open Check, paste a public GitHub repo and press Pop the hood: your browser asks GitHub and four lights answer.

What you can do

  • Check a repo. Paste any public repo and read its four lights. No account, no key. How a check runs.
  • Log in with your wallet. No email, no password, no signature to get in. How to log in.
  • Sign your repo. One small file names your wallet and your coin. How to sign.
  • Launch a coin. Paste your repo, then three clicks: log in, sign, launch. How a launch runs.
  • Open a coin. Its page shows the coin and the proof card of the repo it names. How a coin page reads.

Whose contracts? A coin is created by the Pons v2 contracts on Robinhood Chain. Those contracts are unaudited. Udenhood adds no contract of its own and takes no fee. Costs, limits.

Not live yet. The fee vault and UDEN. They are listed, without dates, on In build.
